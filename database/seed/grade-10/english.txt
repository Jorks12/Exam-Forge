@tenses|Времена и последователност
01|1|Choose the completion: By the time I arrived, the film ___.|had started|starts|has started|is starting|The past perfect places the start of the film before another past event, the speaker's arrival.
02|2|Which sentence describes a habit in the past that has changed?|We used to cycle to school.|We are used to cycling to school.|We have just cycled to school.|We will be cycling to school.|“Used to” followed by the base verb describes a former habit. “Be used to” means being accustomed to something.
@conditionals|Условни изречения
01|2|Choose the completion for advice about the present: If I were you, I ___ the receipt.|would keep|will keep|had kept|would have kept|The second conditional uses a past form in the if-clause and would with the base verb to express hypothetical present advice.
02|3|Which sentence expresses a past decision with a consequence now?|If we had booked earlier, we would have seats now.|If we book earlier, we will have seats tomorrow.|If we booked earlier, we would have seats tomorrow.|If we had booked earlier, we would have had seats yesterday.|The past perfect identifies a past condition, while “would have seats now” describes its hypothetical present consequence.

@time-markers|Time expressions and verb forms
p01|1|Complete: “I have lived here ___ 2021.”|since|for|during|by|Use since before a starting point in time.
p02|1|Complete: “She has worked at the library ___ three years.”|for|since|until|at|Use for with a duration.
p03|1|Which time expression fits “We met at the station ___” in a completed past account?|yesterday|next Tuesday|tomorrow|soon|Yesterday names a finished past period.
p04|2|Complete: “When the bus finally arrived, the last passenger ___.”|had already left|has already left|is already leaving|will have left|Past perfect marks the earlier of two past events.
p05|2|Choose the form for an activity taking place at this very moment: “Leo ___ his bike.”|is repairing|has repaired|had repaired|will repair|Present continuous describes an action in progress.
p06|2|Choose the form for experience up to now: “I ___ Iceland.”|have never visited|never visit yesterday|had never visited before that trip|will never visit tomorrow|Present perfect connects experience to the present.
p07|3|Complete: “On Monday she said she ___ outside the station at six the next day.”|would be waiting|will have waited|has been waiting|is waited|Future continuous backshifts to would be plus the participle.
p08|3|Complete: “By the time the visitors arrive tomorrow, the team ___ the safety checks.”|will have completed|would complete last week|had completed yesterday|has been completed|Future perfect describes completion before a future point.
@grammar-functions|Grammar in use
p01|1|Complete: “The guide ___ helped us was very patient.”|who|where|when|whose|Who refers to a person and functions as subject.
p02|1|Choose the standard question: “How ___ luggage may I take?”|much|many|a few|several|Luggage is uncountable in this meaning.
p03|1|Which word completes “___ of the two doors are locked” if neither can be opened?|both|Neither|Either|None|Both includes the two members of a pair.
p04|2|How can a speaker express regret about failing to check the date yesterday?|should have checked|must check tomorrow|has to check now|might check next week|Should have plus a past participle evaluates a past action.
p05|2|Complete the passive: “The notice ___ by the secretary last Friday.”|was written|wrote|has written|is writing|Past passive uses was with the past participle.
p06|2|Complete: “The teacher reminded us ___ our notebooks.”|to bring|bring|to bringing|brought|Remind takes a person followed by a to-infinitive.
p07|3|Which word means “except if” in “You cannot enter ___ you show a ticket”?|unless|although|because|while|Unless introduces the exception to the restriction.
p08|3|Complete the unreal past condition: “If she ___ earlier, we could have met her.”|had called|calls|will call|would call|A past perfect if-clause marks a condition that did not happen.
