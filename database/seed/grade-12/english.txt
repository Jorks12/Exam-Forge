@grammar|Граматика
01|1|Complete the sentence: If the library is open tomorrow, I ___ the book.|will return|returned|would have returned|had returned|Use will in the main clause of a first conditional about a possible future event.
02|1|Complete the sentence: She ___ here since September and still works here.|has worked|worked|had worked|will work|Since September connects the start of the activity with the present.
03|2|Complete the sentence: If I had known about the delay, I ___ a later train.|would have taken|will take|would take|had taken|A third conditional uses past perfect in the if-clause and would have plus a past participle in the result.
04|2|Complete the sentence: The results ___ by the research team next Monday.|will be published|will publish|have published|were publishing|The results receive the action, so the future passive is required.
05|2|Choose the correct reported statement: “I am leaving now,” Maya said.|Maya said that she was leaving then.|Maya said that I am leaving now.|Maya said that she had leave then.|Maya said that she leaving then.|Reported speech shifts the pronoun, tense and time reference in this context.
06|1|Complete the sentence: This is the student ___ won the debate.|who|which|where|whose|Who is the subject of the relative clause and refers to a person.
07|2|Complete the sentence: The author, ___ latest novel won a prize, will visit our school.|whose|whom|which|where|Whose shows that the novel belongs to the author.
08|2|Choose the best deduction: His coat is gone and he is not in the office. He ___ left already.|must have|must to have|should to have|must be|Must have plus a past participle expresses a strong inference about the past.
09|2|Complete the sentence: You ___ bring food. Lunch is included.|do not have to|must not|could not|had not to|Do not have to expresses the absence of an obligation.
10|1|Complete the sentence: I look forward to ___ from you.|hearing|hear|heard|to hear|To is a preposition in look forward to, so it is followed by an -ing form.
@usage|Езикови конструкции
11|2|Complete the sentence: By the time the guests arrived, we ___ the tables.|had set|have set|will set|were set|The past perfect marks an action completed before another past event.
12|2|Complete the sentence: I wish I ___ more time to practise now.|had|will have|have|had had|Wish plus a past form can describe a present situation the speaker would like to change.
13|3|Complete the sentence: Rarely ___ such a detailed report from a first-year student.|have I read|I have read|did I have read|I read have|A negative or restrictive adverb at the front can trigger subject–auxiliary inversion.
14|2|Complete the sentence: Neither of the proposed dates ___ convenient for me.|is|have|be|are being|In standard formal usage here, neither is followed by the singular verb is.
15|1|Complete the sentence: We need ___ information before we decide.|some|an|many|a few|Information is an uncountable noun and can be used with some.
16|1|Complete the sentence: There were ___ cars on the road today than yesterday.|fewer|less|little|least|Fewer is used with a countable plural noun.
17|2|Complete the sentence: Despite ___ tired, Leo finished the assignment.|being|he was|to be|was|Despite is followed here by an -ing form, not a finite clause.
18|2|Complete the sentence: I am having my laptop ___ this afternoon.|repaired|repair|repairs|to repairing|Have something done describes arranging for another person to do the work.
19|3|Complete the sentence: The speaker avoided ___ the question directly.|answering|to answer|answer|answered|Avoid takes a gerund complement.
20|3|Complete the sentence: By this time next week, we ___ the survey.|will have completed|would complete|have completed|had completed|The future perfect marks completion before a specified future time.

35|2|Which connector best completes “The route is longer. ___, it is safer at night”?|However|Therefore|For instance|Similarly|However signals a contrast between the disadvantage and the advantage.
36|2|Which connector best completes “The bridge was closed. ___, we took the bus”?|As a result|In contrast|Meanwhile|For example|As a result introduces the consequence of the closure.
37|2|Which email subject is clearest for asking about enrolment deadlines?|Question about the enrolment deadline|Hello from me|Please read this|A little thing|A specific subject line lets the recipient identify the purpose immediately.
38|3|What does “We can proceed provided that everyone agrees” mean?|We can proceed only on condition that everyone agrees.|We will proceed even if nobody agrees.|We agreed because we had already proceeded.|We must prevent people from agreeing.|Provided that introduces a condition.
39|2|Which sentence makes a tentative suggestion?|We might consider starting half an hour later.|Start half an hour later immediately.|We definitely started later yesterday.|Nobody is allowed to suggest a time.|Might consider leaves the proposal open for discussion.
40|3|Which sentence best separates an observation from an inference?|Attendance fell after the schedule changed, but we cannot yet establish the cause.|The schedule caused the decline because both happened on Tuesday.|Attendance did not change at all, so the schedule must be better.|The new schedule is obviously a disaster.|The sentence reports the timing and acknowledges that causation has not been established.
