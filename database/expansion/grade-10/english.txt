@tenses|Времена и последователност
exp-01|1|Complete the routine: My brother usually ___ the dog before breakfast.|walks|walk|walking|has walk|In the simple present, a third-person singular subject takes walks.
exp-02|1|Complete the sentence about a finished event: We ___ the final match last Saturday.|watched|watch|will watch|are watching|Last Saturday places the event in a completed past period.
exp-03|1|Which form completes this promise? “I ___ you a copy when I get home.”|will send|have sent|had sent|sending|Will plus the base verb can express a promise about a future action.
exp-04|2|Complete the interrupted action: “I ___ home when my phone rang.”|was walking|have walked|will walk|had walk|The past continuous presents walking as in progress when a shorter event occurred.
exp-05|2|Choose the form for a confirmed future arrangement: “We ___ our tutor at ten tomorrow. The appointment is in the calendar.”|are meeting|met|had met|meet yesterday|The present continuous can describe a future arrangement already made.
exp-06|2|Which question asks about an experience at any time up to now?|Have you ever made bread?|Did you make bread yesterday?|Are you making bread right now?|Will you make bread tomorrow?|Have you ever uses the present perfect to ask about experience without naming a finished time.
exp-07|2|Complete the sentence about a change before now: “The weather ___ colder over the last few days and it is still getting worse.”|has become|had become before last June|will become tomorrow|become|The present perfect connects the recent change to the current situation.
exp-08|2|Which option makes it clear that the second action began immediately after the first finished?|As soon as the lesson ended, the students left.|While the lesson was in progress, the students left.|Before the lesson ended, the students left.|Long after the lesson ended, the students left.|As soon as places the departure immediately after the end of the lesson.
exp-09|3|A teacher says, “The printer was working when I left, but now it won't start.” Which paraphrase preserves the sequence?|The printer worked at departure and has since stopped working.|The printer stopped before the teacher left and now works again.|The printer was broken at departure and is still broken.|The teacher left only after repairing a printer that still works.|Was working fixes the earlier state, while now it won't start describes a later change.
exp-10|3|Which report correctly preserves both past reference points? “By Tuesday, I had sent the draft. On Wednesday, I changed the title.”|The draft had already been sent before the Wednesday title change.|The draft was sent after the title was changed.|Both actions took place on Wednesday.|The writer has not yet sent the draft.|The draft was already sent by Tuesday, so sending preceded the Wednesday change.
@conditionals|Условни изречения
exp-11|1|Complete this description of how the device normally works: “If you press the green button, the screen ___ up.”|lights|would have lit|lit yesterday|had lit|The sentence describes a recurring result, so both clauses use the simple present.
exp-12|1|Which word means “if not” in “We can walk there unless it rains”?|unless|there|can|walk|Unless introduces an exception and is equivalent to if it does not in this context.
exp-13|1|Which sentence sets a condition for using a room?|You may use the room if it is free.|You used the room yesterday.|The room has four windows.|The room is beside the office.|The if-clause makes permission depend on availability.
exp-14|2|Complete the hypothetical statement: “If I ___ more storage space, I would keep the old boxes.”|had|have|will have|have had|Past simple in the if-clause marks a hypothetical present situation.
exp-15|2|Which rewrite preserves “Without a password, you cannot open the document”?|If you do not have a password, you cannot open the document.|If you have a password, you cannot open the document.|You can open the document if you forget the password.|You must open the document before creating a password.|Without a password means in the condition of having no password.
exp-16|2|Complete the safety instruction: “If the alarm sounds, ___ the building by the nearest exit.”|leave|left|would leave yesterday|had left|An imperative in the main clause tells the reader what action to take when the condition is met.
exp-17|2|Choose the ending that expresses a possibility, rather than a definite outcome: “If the fog clears, we ___ see the lighthouse.”|might|must not|did|had|Might presents visibility as possible once the condition is satisfied.
exp-18|2|Which statement means the person can borrow the bicycle on condition that they bring it back by noon?|You can borrow the bicycle as long as you return it by noon.|You can borrow it because you returned it yesterday.|You can borrow it even if you return it after noon.|You cannot borrow it until someone returns it tomorrow.|As long as introduces the condition under which permission applies.
exp-19|3|Which option expresses both parts of “If you find the pass, bring it. Otherwise, call reception”?|Bring the pass if you find it, and call reception if you do not.|Call reception only after you bring the pass.|Bring the pass even if you cannot find it.|Call reception before looking for the pass in every case.|Otherwise refers to the opposite condition, in which the pass is not found.
exp-20|3|Which statement follows from “The boat will sail only if the wind drops,” when the wind stays strong?|The boat will not sail.|The boat will sail at once.|The boat must already have sailed.|The wind drops whenever the boat sails.|Only if makes weaker wind necessary for sailing. When it remains strong, the requirement is unmet.

exp-40|3|What is the difference between “underpaid” and “unpaid” in these job advertisements?|Underpaid means paid too little.
