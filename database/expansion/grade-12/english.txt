@grammar|Граматика
exp-01|1|Choose the correct pronoun: Emma made the model by ___.|herself|her|she|hers|By herself means that Emma did the work without help.
exp-02|1|Complete the directions: Walk ___ the tunnel to reach the station on the other side.|through|above|beside|among|Through describes movement inside a passage from one end to the other.
exp-03|1|Complete the statement: This route is ___ than the motorway at rush hour.|quicker|quickest|as quick|more quickest|Than introduces a comparison, so the comparative form quicker is required.
exp-04|2|The parcels were delivered, but I was not there when they ___.|arrived|have arrived|are arriving|arrive|Both events are in a finished past context, so arrived is the appropriate simple past form.
exp-05|2|Choose the sentence that describes a former habit.|I used to walk home after school.|I am used to walking home after school.|I am getting used to walking home after school.|I used the path to walk home after school.|Used to plus the base verb describes a past habit, whereas be used to describes familiarity.
exp-06|2|Complete the sentence: We are considering ___ the deadline.|extending|to extending|extend|extended|Consider takes an -ing complement when it means thinking about a possible action.
exp-07|2|Complete the sentence: Every participant ___ given a numbered badge at the door yesterday.|was|were|has|have|Every participant is singular and yesterday fixes the past passive form was given.
exp-08|2|Choose the form that indicates an action in progress at 8 p.m. yesterday: At 8 p.m., I ___ the presentation.|was rehearsing|have rehearsed|will be rehearsing|had already rehearsed|The past continuous presents the rehearsal as ongoing at the reference time.
exp-09|3|Which sentence describes a present consequence of failing to save a file earlier?|If I had saved the file, I would have a copy now.|If I save the file, I will have a copy tomorrow.|If I had saved the file, I would have sent it yesterday.|If I save the file now, I can send it later.|A past perfect condition combines with a present result, signalled by would have a copy now.
exp-10|3|Which rewrite preserves the meaning of “Only when the lights went out did we notice the storm”?|We did not notice the storm until the lights went out.|We noticed the storm before the lights went out.|We noticed that the storm had stopped when the lights went out.|The lights went out only after we noticed the storm.|Only when restricts the time of noticing to the point when the lights went out.
@usage|Езикови конструкции
exp-11|1|Complete the sentence: I borrowed a charger ___ my neighbour.|from|at|about|on|Borrow something from someone identifies the person who lends it.
exp-12|1|Choose the natural question about a trip's duration.|How long did the journey take?|How much long was the journey?|How many did the journey take?|How far in time did it take?|How long asks about duration and did is followed by the base form take.
exp-13|1|Complete the notice: Staff are responsible ___ checking the equipment.|for|with|to|about|Responsible for introduces a duty and can be followed by an -ing form.
exp-14|2|Choose the sentence in which “stop” means interrupting one activity in order to do another.|We stopped to buy water on the way home.|We stopped buying bottled water last year.|We stopped the car outside the shop.|We stopped the water from leaking.|Stop to buy means pausing one activity for the purpose of buying something.
exp-15|2|Complete the sentence: Please remind me ___ the hall before Friday.|to book|booking|booked|to booking|The pattern remind someone to do something calls for a to-infinitive.
exp-16|2|Complete the sentence: The instructions are clear enough for a beginner ___.|to follow|follow|following|followed|Enough for someone to do something expresses a sufficient degree for the action.
exp-17|2|Which option reports a question with the correct word order?|She asked where the tickets were.|She asked where were the tickets.|She asked where did the tickets be.|She asked where the tickets was.|An embedded question uses statement order, and plural tickets takes were.
exp-18|2|What does “He is accustomed to working late” mean?|Working late feels normal to him.|He has just stopped working late.|He will never work late again.|He is required to work late today.|Be accustomed to describes familiarity with an activity, followed by the -ing form.
exp-19|3|Which version keeps the meaning of “You didn't need to print the slides, but you did”?|You needn't have printed the slides.|You didn't print the slides because you didn't need to.|You mustn't print the slides.|You couldn't have printed the slides.|Needn't have plus a past participle says the action happened although it was unnecessary.
exp-20|3|Which option correctly completes “Not until the second trial ___ that the sensor had been fitted backwards”?|did the team realise|the team did realise|the team realised|had the team realise|Fronted not until triggers inversion in the main clause. With did, the main verb stays in its base form.
@lexis|Лексика

exp-22|1|If a product is durable, what can you expect?|It can withstand use for a long time.|It is only available in one colour.|It must be replaced immediately.|It weighs almost nothing.|Durable describes something that remains usable despite wear over time.
exp-23|1|Which verb means to give money or goods to a cause without payment in return?|Donate|Borrow|Charge|Refund|To donate is to give voluntarily, often to a charity or public cause.
exp-24|2|Choose the natural collocation: The report ___ attention to a gap in the records.|draws|catches|makes|takes|Draw attention to something means direct people's notice to it.
exp-25|2|In “The two estimates differ by a narrow margin,” what does margin mean?|The size of the difference|The source of the estimates|A missing page|The total of the two estimates|A narrow margin means the difference is small.
exp-26|2|Which verb fits “The tenants voted to ___ the existing rules for another year”?|retain|retrieve|release|remove|Retain means keep in force or keep possession of something already held.
exp-27|2|What does “follow up on an enquiry” mean?|Take further action regarding the enquiry|Keep the enquiry secret forever|Submit an enquiry on an unrelated subject|Forget the enquiry altogether|To follow up is to take a later step in response to a previous contact or action.
exp-28|2|Which word means based on personal judgement rather than a common measurable standard?|Subjective|Identical|Random|Comprehensive|Subjective assessments depend on an individual's perspective or judgement.
exp-29|3|A reviewer calls an explanation “plausible but unsubstantiated”. What is the reviewer saying?|It could be true, but supporting evidence is missing.|It is supported by evidence, so it must be true.|It is impossible to understand because it is long.|It is demonstrably false because it was tested.|Plausible means believable, while unsubstantiated means lacking adequate supporting evidence.
exp-30|3|What does “qualified support” mean when a committee backs a plan only if spending is capped?|Support limited by a stated condition|Support from professionally certified people only|An unconditional endorsement|An outright refusal to discuss the plan|In this context qualified means restricted by a reservation or condition.

exp-32|1|Which phrase introduces a specific illustration of a general point?|For example|In conclusion|On the contrary|At last|For example signals that a concrete instance of the previous point follows.

exp-37|2|Choose the most precise subject line for reporting that a school portal rejects a valid password.|School portal rejects valid password|Important things|Question from a student|An update about something online|A precise subject line names the system and the problem.
